Lesson 03 / Shadow

Let light explain the form.

Shadow tells the viewer where the light is, how the object turns, and where it touches the world around it.

Shadow and light

A shadow appears when an object blocks light. In animation, consistent shadow design keeps characters and environments grounded, dimensional, and readable.

Objectives

  • Identify the direction and size of a light source.
  • Separate highlight, half-tone, core shadow, and reflected light.
  • Place a cast shadow that supports the object’s contact and weight.

Form shadow

The form shadow is the part of the object turning away from the light. On a sphere it changes gradually; on a cube it shifts plane by plane.

Core and reflected light

The core shadow is usually the darkest area on the object. Reflected light can soften the far edge, but it should remain darker than the lit side.

Cast shadow

The cast shadow falls onto another surface. Its direction follows the light source, and its darkest point is usually where the object meets the ground.

Practice prompt

Draw one sphere and one box under the same lamp. Label the highlight, half-tone, core shadow, reflected light, and cast shadow.
