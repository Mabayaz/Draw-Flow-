Stage one / unlocked

Form and volume

Turn flat marks into objects with weight. Start with the primitive forms that support almost every character, prop, and environment.

Ready to practice
Cube
Sphere
Cylinder

Exercise A: Primitive inventory

Draw a cube, sphere, and cylinder from three angles. Add a centerline to each one so the volume remains clear when it turns.

Exercise B: Combine the forms

Build a simple object from at least two primitives. Keep the overlaps visible and check that the parts share the same scale.

Complete both studies before moving to perspective.

Perspective and space

Use horizon lines and vanishing points to place your forms inside a believable world.

Locked

One-point practice Draw a box facing you. Send every depth edge toward one vanishing point on the horizon.
Two-point practice Turn the box. Establish left and right vanishing points before drawing its top and bottom edges.
