The practice room

Make looking a habit.

Choose a small constraint, set a timer, and let your attention do the work. Apply your learning through practice activities and the Self-Check assessment.

Daily exercise / 01

Notice what repeats.

Spend ten minutes finding one repeated shape, line, or color in the room around you. Draw only that pattern to build your spatial awareness and form control.

10:00

Small rules

  • 01. Keep the tool simple.
  • 02. Stop before the idea gets comfortable.
  • 03. Write one sentence about what you noticed.
