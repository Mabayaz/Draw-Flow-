ABOUT US[cite: 1]

Learn. Practice. Improve.[cite: 1]

DrawFlow is a supplementary educational website created to support Animation students in learning and practicing Solid Drawing[cite: 1]. It provides lessons, visual examples, instructional videos, and interactive exercises covering Form, Perspective, Shadow, and Depth[cite: 1]. Our goal is to provide a simple and engaging learning space where students can develop their drawing skills step by step[cite: 1].
