The idea behind DrawFlow

Attention is a creative tool.

DrawFlow is a quiet studio for developing the habit of looking closely. The work is small on purpose: a few minutes, one constraint, a clearer eye.

Our approach

Less performance. More noticing.

We design exercises that make observation concrete. Instead of waiting for inspiration, you begin with a question and give your attention somewhere to land.

Progress here is not measured by polish. It is measured by the details you notice today that you would have missed yesterday.

Field note

“The eye is a muscle. Give it interesting things to lift.”
