Lesson 04 / Depth

Make a flat scene feel deep.

Depth gives the viewer scale, focus, movement, and a reason to believe the space your animation occupies.

Depth in animation

Believable 2D space comes from several cues working together: perspective, overlap, relative scale, atmospheric change, and motion between layers.

Objectives

  • Use scale and overlap to organize foreground, middle ground, and background.
  • Apply parallax and multiplane thinking to moving scenes.
  • Use atmospheric perspective to soften distant forms.

Depth of field

Depth of field controls which parts of a scene feel sharp or soft. A shallow field can focus attention; a deep field can make the whole environment feel present.

Parallax and layers

When a camera moves, nearby layers appear to move faster than distant layers. Separating artwork into planes creates a convincing multiplane effect.

Atmospheric perspective

Distance reduces detail, contrast, and color intensity. Distant forms often become lighter, cooler, and less defined than the foreground.

Practice prompt

Design a three-layer landscape. Make the foreground sharp and high contrast, the middle ground calmer, and the background lighter and less detailed.
