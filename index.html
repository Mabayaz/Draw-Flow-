What is Solid Drawing?[cite: 1]

The skill of conveying a 3D character or object in a 2D drawing[cite: 1].

It emphasizes drawing what will appear solid, balanced, and believable by demonstrating form, weight, proportion, structure, and depth[cite: 1].
